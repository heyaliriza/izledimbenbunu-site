Delete your account and data

You can permanently delete your “İzledim Ben Bunu” account at any time.

Inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password and confirm the deletion.

If you cannot access the app

Send an email from the address you registered with to alirizahd@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request”. After verifying your identity we will delete your account within 30 days at the latest.

What gets deleted?

  • Your email address, username and password data
  • All your reviews and ratings
  • Your lists and watch history
  • All your sessions and access keys on your devices

Deletion is irreversible. Database backups are fully erased within 30 days at the latest, following the hosting provider's automatic backup cycle.
