I Watched This

Track the series, movies and anime you watch, and go beyond a single overall rating: review each season and each episode separately.

What can you do?

  • Search series, movies and anime and mark what you have watched.
  • Write a rating and review for a movie, a series, every season and every episode.
  • Mark reviews that contain spoilers so they stay hidden from others.
  • Keep your watch status: plan to watch, watching, completed, dropped.

The app is free and has no ads.

Data source

Series and movie information (posters, summaries, season and episode lists) is provided by TMDB. This app uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.
