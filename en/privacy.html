Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains which personal data the “I Watched This” (Turkish: “İzledim Ben Bunu”) mobile app (the “App”) collects, why it is used, and what your rights are.

1. Data controller

Ali Rıza Uzunoğlu (developer). Contact: alirizahd@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

DataPurpose
Email addressCreating an account, signing in, and account-related communication
UsernameShowing your account and reviews to other users
Password (only as an irreversible hash)Authentication. Your password is never stored in plain text.
Watch history, lists, ratings and reviewsThe core function of the App
Notification token (optional)Sending new-episode alerts. Only if you turn on Settings → New episode alerts, your device’s notification token (Expo Push) and language are stored on our server; they are deleted when you turn it off, sign out or delete your account. Notifications are delivered through Expo and Google (FCM) infrastructure.
Profile photo (optional)Showing it on your profile. The photo you choose is resized to 256×256 on your device, uploaded to our server and visible to everyone regardless of profile privacy. You can remove it any time; it is also deleted with your account.
Profile bio, your own lists and your privacy settingShowing your profile and lists to other users. While your profile is public, your username appears in search and your watch list, reviews and public lists can be seen. With a private profile only you can see them (your username still appears in search, but your content does not).
Moderation recordsWhen a review is removed or an account is suspended, the decision, the reason and the text of the removed review are kept so that appeals can be assessed. These records are deleted when you delete your account.
Report and block recordsKeeping the community safe. When you report a review, your account, the reason and (if you wrote one) your description are recorded; the reported person is never told who reported them. The list of users you have blocked is also stored.
Session information (refresh token)So that you do not have to sign in every time you open the App. It is kept in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore).
Language preferenceStored on your device only, to show the App in your chosen language. The language is also sent with requests so that titles and summaries come back in that language.

We do not collect advertising IDs, location, contacts, camera or microphone data. We show no ads and we do not sell your data.

3. How data is used and shared

  • Your data is used only to run the App.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.
  • Your reviews and username may be visible to other users, depending on your privacy settings. The text of reviews you mark as spoilers is not sent to users who have not watched that title.
  • Series and movie information comes from TMDB. These requests are made through our server; none of your personal data is shared with TMDB.
  • Your data is stored with the hosting provider that runs the App's server infrastructure: not yet determined (this section will be updated before the App is released).

4. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed with a strong algorithm (argon2id). Session keys are stored encrypted on the device.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account inside the App (Settings → Delete my account) or by following the steps on the account deletion page. When you delete your account, the account and all data linked to it (reviews, ratings, lists, watch history, sessions) are removed permanently and irreversibly. Automatic database backups are fully erased within 30 days at the latest, following the hosting provider's backup cycle.

6. Your rights

Under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and related legislation, you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to its processing. You can send your requests to the email address above.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. Important changes are announced inside the App. The current version is always on this page.

9. TMDB attribution
